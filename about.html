Our Story

The ultimate casual Greek dining destination on Poath Rd, Hughesdale.

Hellenic Depot is your local destination for an authentic, high-end casual Greek dining experience. Located in the heart of Hughesdale, Melbourne, we serve up a rich variety of hot appetisers, fresh seafood, and traditional marinated pork, chicken & lamb gyro cooked over hot charcoal.

Couple this with our unique bar dressed in authentic railway timber sleepers, offering a curated selection of Greek & local wines, craft beers on tap, and signature cocktails designed to excite your senses.
